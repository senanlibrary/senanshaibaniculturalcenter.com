---
title: "Book discussion at UT Austin with Muhsin al-Musawi"
date: 2026-11-02
summary: "The center co-sponsored a discussion of Muhsin al-Musawi's Poetic Desire and Literary Thievery."
---
On Friday, October 16, the center co-sponsored a book discussion at the University of Texas at Austin on *Poetic Desire and Literary Thievery: Economies of Intertextuality in Arabic Literature* by Muhsin J. al-Musawi of Columbia University. Tarek El-Ariss of Dartmouth College was the discussant.

The event was held at noon in GAR 4.100. Co-sponsors were the Institute for Historical Studies, the Humanities Institute, and the UT Center for Middle Eastern Studies.
