The center's holdings comprise more than 20,000 books, journals, and
newspapers in Arabic and English, including rare periodicals, Arab American
diaspora publications, and archival material held in few other places in the
United States. Items are consultable on site in Lincoln, Texas.

The subject guides below describe particular strengths of the collection in
detail. For everything cataloged to date, search the catalog.
